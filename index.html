Paper Plane Race Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

Paper Plane Race (the game) is published by DRIM LLC. This policy explains what information the game uses.

Information we collect

The game saves your progress on your device. We use Firebase Analytics to collect anonymous usage data such as app opens, levels played, purchases and ad views, along with a random app instance ID and basic device information (model, OS version, country). We do not ask for your name, email or precise location.

Ads

The game shows ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may use an advertising ID and similar identifiers to show and measure ads. Where required, we ask for your consent first. You can reset or limit your advertising ID in your Android settings.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Google Play. We do not see or store your payment details.

Children

The game is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Data sharing and retention

Analytics and ad data is processed by Google under its own privacy policy (policies.google.com/privacy). We do not sell personal data. To ask about or delete data tied to your device, contact us.
